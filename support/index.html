Spin Streak Support

Need help with Spin Streak, want to report a problem, or have a privacy request?

Email akos@famescale.co. Please include your device model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of what happened. Do not email passwords, Apple credentials, or player link codes.

Common questions

How do I start?
Sign in with Apple, create or recover your player profile, then tap New Game. Choose a format and at least two players.

What is a Friend Code?
Share it with another Spin Streak user so they can add your account as a player.

What is a Player Link Code?
It transfers a local player profile and its connected history to that player's own Apple-authenticated account. Treat the code as private and share it only with the intended player.

How do I delete my account?
Open your profile in the app, choose Delete Account, and confirm. You may also contact the email above for assistance.
